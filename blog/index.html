Insights

Commentary on developments in residential and leasehold property, written for landlords, freeholders and management companies. Short, specific, and about the things that decide cases rather than the things that make headlines.

MV/N-021
Possession
8 September 2026

Ground 8 now needs three months, not two

The mandatory rent arrears ground has been reset. The threshold rose from two months to three, and the notice period doubled. Both change when a landlord can act.
MV/N-020
Possession
7 September 2026

Section 21 is gone. What actually changed on 1 May

Section 21 could last be served on 30 April 2026. On 1 May every assured shorthold tenancy converted to an assured periodic tenancy. The consequences run wider than evictions.
MV/N-019
Compliance
5 September 2026

The information sheet, and the landlords who have not served it

From 31 May 2026 landlords must give tenants the government's Renters' Rights Act information sheet. It is a small obligation with a long shadow.
MV/N-018
Litigation
3 September 2026

Twenty-seven weeks, and what it should change about your approach

Ministry of Justice figures put the median time from possession claim to repossession at 27.1 weeks. That number should govern how arrears are handled long before a claim is issued.
MV/N-017
Leasehold
1 September 2026

Standardised service charge demands are coming. Most blocks are not ready

The service charge transparency reforms will require standardised demand forms, annual reports and standardised accounts. The lead-in is twelve months, and it starts when the regulations land.
