How to install it in Gmail

  1. Select the whole signature block above — click just before the logo and drag to the end of the small print.
  2. Copy it (Cmd + C).
  3. In Gmail: Settings (gear icon) → See all settings → General tab.
  4. Scroll to Signature → Create new → name it Marchbank & Vale.
  5. Click into the editing box and paste (Cmd + V).
  6. Underneath, set both dropdowns — For new emails use and On reply/forward use — to that signature.
  7. Scroll to the bottom and click Save Changes.

Important: leave “Insert signature before quoted text” unticked unless you prefer it that way — and do not tick “remove the ‘--’ line”, which does not apply here.

On “interactive” signatures

Gmail and Outlook strip JavaScript and most modern CSS, so hover effects and embedded widgets either fail silently or render as broken boxes — particularly in Outlook and on mobile. The signatures that look impressive in a showcase are usually screenshots, or they behave that way in only the one client they were filmed in.

What does work everywhere is a link to a file. Save our details downloads a vCard: one tap on a phone and Marchbank & Vale is in the recipient’s address book, with both addresses, the office and the statutory wording already attached. For someone holding an arrears letter that is worth more than an animation, and it will still work in ten years.
